PharmaMind ERP + AI is an enterprise-grade **multi-tenant SaaS ERP platform** designed for pharmaceutical businesses.

The goal is simple:

> Transform pharmaceutical operations using intelligent automation and predictive AI.


### 🧠 AI-Powered Features

```text
🤖 Stockout Prediction
   Predict medicines that may run out soon.

💊 Expiry Intelligence
   Identify medicines at risk of expiry.

📈 Sales Forecasting
   Predict future demand.

🏪 Smart Inventory Management
   Optimize stock levels automatically.

💰 Customer Payment Intelligence
   Identify payment risks.

🚚 Delivery Optimization
   Improve pharmaceutical logistics.
```
